Chromatica

Live

Six centuries of painting reduced to the colours they're made of: 34,062 colours extracted from 7,471 paintings in five open-access museum collections, from 1309 to 1910. Every particle is a real colour and leads back to its painting.

What it shows: the most saturated painting in six centuries is the earliest. Dutch painters are the most muted, and the 19th century only half recovers.

Internet Adoption Map

Live

30 years of the internet (1990–2024) as a timelapse with the milestones that shaped it: how long each country took to go from 10% to 50% of people online. 13 data layers, a 3D globe and data refreshed every month.

What it shows: it wasn't the latecomers who moved fastest, it was the rich. The slowest were middle-income countries that stalled for a decade.
